# Furniture & home

## Retailers
- **Established brands:** Hatil, Otobi, Regal, Akhtar Furnishers, Brothers Furniture, Partex, Navana. Most have their own online stores and showrooms.
- **Daraz** for small items and flat-pack.
- **Facebook pages** for custom/made-to-order work. See the Facebook rules in SKILL.md.
- **Bikroy** for used furniture. Listings commonly claim "original Hatil/Otobi"; verify in person.

## Evidence
- Material: solid wood vs. MDF/particle board vs. veneer. Ask the seller to state it explicitly.
- Joinery and hardware quality.
- Owner reports on durability in humid conditions (warping, swelling, termites).

## Check
- Dimensions vs. room, doorways, stairs, and lift size.
- Delivery and assembly cost. Delivery to upper floors without a lift may cost extra.
- Lead time for made-to-order.
- Termite and moisture treatment for wood.
- Warranty or after-sales repair terms.

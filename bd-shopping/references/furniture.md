# Furniture, mattresses & home

## Routes
- **Established brands:** Hatil, Otobi, Regal, Akhtar Furnishers, Brothers Furniture, Partex, Navana. Showrooms and own online stores. Listed prices are negotiable.
- **Local carpenter (made to order):** a large part of the market and often the best value for solid wood. Price comes from a quote, not a listing. The research job is to give the user the right questions and a materials spec, not a price.
- **Daraz** for small items and flat-pack.
- **Facebook pages** for custom work. Follow the Facebook rules in SKILL.md.
- **Bikroy** for used furniture. "Original Hatil/Otobi" claims need in-person verification.

## Wood — the main quality signal
- Ask for the **species**, not just "solid wood": segun (teak), mehogoni (mahogany), gamari, koroi, shil koroi, chambal, and others. Teak and mehogoni sit at the top of price and durability; the cheaper species vary.
- **Fake "segun" is common.** A cheaper wood stained to look like teak, or a teak veneer over board. Ask whether it's solid throughout, and check the wood seasoning and treatment claims.
- Board products: MDF, particle board, plywood, with veneer or laminate on top. Fine at the right price, but they swell when wet. State the material explicitly in the Brief.

## Evidence
- Owner reports on durability in humid conditions: warping, swelling, termites, hardware failure.
- Joinery and hardware photos.

## Check
- Dimensions vs. room, doorways, stairs, and lift.
- Delivery and assembly cost. Upper floors without a lift may cost extra.
- Lead time for made-to-order, and whether an advance is refundable.
- Termite and moisture treatment.
- Warranty or after-sales repair terms.

## Carpenter quotes — what to specify
Wood species (solid throughout or veneer), seasoning, treatment, finish, hardware brand, dimensions drawing, delivery and fitting, advance amount and what happens if the delivered piece doesn't match the spec.

## Mattresses
Material (foam density, spring type, coir), firmness, size in inches matched to the bed frame, cover fabric, warranty on sagging. Trial or return terms are rare, so in-store testing matters.

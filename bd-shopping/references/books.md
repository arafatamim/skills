# Books & media

## Retailers
- **Rokomari** — largest online bookshop; broad local and foreign catalogue.
- **BoiBazar**, **Batighar (Baatighar)**, **PBS**, **Prothoma** — alternatives with strong local catalogues.
- **Wafilife** — Islamic books.
- **Daraz** — large book catalogue from multiple sellers.

## Check
- **Original vs. pirated print.** Common for foreign titles. Check paper and print quality in reviews and photos. A foreign title far below the usual price is a red flag.
- Edition and translation.
- Delivery time and fee.
- Offer an ebook if it's cheaper and acceptable to the user.

## Timing
Amar Ekushey Boi Mela (February) brings new releases and fair discounts on local titles.

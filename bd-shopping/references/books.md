# Books & media

## Retailers
- **Rokomari** — largest online bookshop; broad local and foreign catalogue.
- **BoiBazar**, **Batighar (Baatighar)**, **PBS (Pathak Shamabesh)**, **Prothoma** — alternatives with strong local catalogues.
- **Wafilife** — Islamic books.
- **Daraz** — large catalogue from many sellers.

## Original vs. local print
- Read the listing's print label: retailers like Rokomari mark foreign titles as **original/imported** vs. **local/Indian print** or similar. Report the label; don't infer from price.
- If there's no label, a foreign title far below the usual price is most likely a local or pirated print. Say so.

## Check
- Edition and translation.
- Delivery time and fee.

## Ebooks
Offer an ebook if it's cheaper and the user is fine with it, but only from legitimate stores (publisher, Google Play Books, Kindle, local legitimate platforms). Never point to pirate sources.

## Timing
Amar Ekushey Boi Mela (February) brings new releases and fair discounts on local titles.

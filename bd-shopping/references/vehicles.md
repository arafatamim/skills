# Motorcycles, bicycles & scooters

## Motorcycles
### Sellers
- Each brand sells through its BD distributor and dealer network. Look up the current distributor on the brand's own BD site. Dealers sell at the distributor's listed price, sometimes with offers.
- Prices are commonly quoted ex-showroom. Registration is extra.

### Real cost
```
ex-showroom price + BRTA registration (and related fees, tax token) + insurance + dealer charges = on-road cost
```
Look up current BRTA registration costs by engine size. They change and they're a large share of the total. If you can't verify them, report on-road cost as UNKNOWN.

### Evidence
Owner reports for mileage, reliability, parts availability and service-center quality in BD. Parts availability and service network matter as much as the bike.

### Check
- Engine-size rules and any registration limits in force (look up current rules).
- Service intervals and the cost of the first paid services.
- Brake type (ABS / CBS) and tyre type.

### Used motorcycles
- Papers: registration certificate in the seller's name, tax token, insurance, and that the engine and chassis numbers match the papers.
- Check for police or theft records where possible, and plan the ownership transfer through BRTA (cost and time).
- Inspect in person, ideally with a mechanic. Never pay before papers are verified.

## Helmets
Only certified helmets (DOT, ECE R22, or another recognised standard). Uncertified "decorative" helmets are common and offer little protection. Don't recommend them at any price.

## Bicycles
- Local brands (e.g., Duranta) and imported brands through dealers. Frame material, size by rider height, brakes, gears.
- Check parts availability for imported models.

## E-scooters and e-bikes
- Look up current legal status and registration requirements before recommending one for road use.
- Battery: chemistry, capacity, replacement cost, charger safety. Avoid unbranded batteries and chargers.

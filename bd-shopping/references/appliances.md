# Home appliances

AC, fridge, washing machine, fan, air cooler, oven, geyser, gas stove, water purifier.

## Retailers
- **Local manufacturers/distributors:** Walton, Singer (also sells Beko), Vision, Jamuna, Transcom Digital / Transcom Electronics (multi-brand, including Daikin and Transtec), Gree (distributed by Electro Mart Group).
- Other brands in the market: Haier, Hisense, Midea, Samsung, LG, Carrier.
- Brand showrooms and authorized dealers first. Daraz official stores second.
- **Listed prices are negotiable** at showrooms and dealers. Report the listed price as a ceiling.

## Evidence
- Energy rating and stated consumption. Inverter vs. non-inverter changes running cost a lot.
- Owner reports on **local service quality**. For appliances, the service network matters more than review scores.

## AC sizing
- 1 ton = 12,000 BTU/h. Check stated BTU and cooling capacity, not the ton label alone: odd sizes like "1.6 ton" are a marketing figure until the BTU confirms it.
- Rough guide only: around 1 ton per 120–150 sq ft for a typical room at normal ceiling height. Size up for a top floor, west-facing walls, large windows, a room next to the kitchen, or many occupants. Cite the brand's own sizing guide alongside this; don't present the rough guide as precise.

## Check
- **Installation cost**, especially for ACs: pipe length, outdoor stand, labour. Find out whether "free installation" has a pipe-length cap.
- **Warranty split:** compressor/motor warranty is often much longer than general parts. Report both.
- **Warranty paperwork:** claims usually need the invoice and warranty card. Record where they're kept in `purchases.jsonl`.
- Whether the brand's service center covers the user's area (delivery area in `shopping/prefs.md`).
- **Power:** voltage fluctuation (stabilizer needed or bundled?), and for load-shedding, whether it can run on the user's IPS/inverter (startup current) and whether surge damage voids the warranty. See `power-backup.md`.

## Safety
Gas geysers, LPG stoves, regulators and hoses: buy certified products from authorized sellers and use authorized installation. Never recommend the cheapest unbranded regulator or hose. Gas-related items are not a place to save money.

## Fit
Room size → AC capacity; household size → fridge/washer capacity; physical dimensions vs. space, doorways, and stairs.

## Timing
AC demand and campaigns peak in spring and early summer (Eid and summer offers: cashback, EMI, free installation). Off-season has less demand but fewer campaigns. Compare against pre-campaign prices in `price_history.jsonl`. Heaters and geysers follow the reverse cycle.

## EMI
Most brands offer EMI through partner banks. Confirm whether it's genuinely 0% or carries a processing fee.

# Gold, silver & jewellery

## The price is mostly set, not shopped
- The **gold rate** per vori/gram for each purity is announced by BAJUS (Bangladesh Jewellers Association) and adjusted as world prices move. Fetch the current rate from BAJUS or a reliable news source and cite its date. Never use a remembered rate.
- 1 vori (bhori) = 11.664 g. State weights in both grams and vori.
- What differs between shops is mostly **making charge (mojuri)**, **wastage/deductions**, and honesty about purity.

## Purity
- Common grades: 22K, 21K, 18K, and "traditional". Purity determines price per gram, so the karat must be written on the receipt.
- Ask for a **hallmark** or purity certificate. Lower-karat gold sold as higher-karat is the main fraud.

## Receipt must itemise
Weight (grams), karat, rate used and its date, making charge, VAT, stone weight deducted from gold weight (if any). Missing items are a red flag.

## Making charge and buyback
- Making charges are negotiable and vary a lot by design and shop.
- **Exchange/buyback deductions:** shops deduct a percentage when you sell or exchange. Ask the rate before buying. It decides whether jewellery works as savings.
- For savings/investment intent, compare against plain bars/coins (lower making charge) and note that this is not financial advice.

## Diamonds and stones
- Natural vs. lab-grown must be stated. Lab-grown is real diamond but has very different resale value.
- Ask for a grading certificate from a recognised lab (GIA, IGI, or similar) and check the report number on the lab's own site.

## Silver
Same structure: rate, purity (e.g., 925), weight, making charge.

## Sellers
Established jewellers with physical shops. Don't buy gold or diamonds from FB pages or classifieds.

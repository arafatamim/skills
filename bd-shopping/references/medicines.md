# Medicines & OTC health products

**Price and availability research only.** Never recommend which medicine to take, a dose, a duration, or whether to start, stop, or switch. Those are decisions for the prescribing doctor or a pharmacist. If the user asks for one, say so and point them there.

## Retailers
- Licensed online pharmacies: **Arogga**, **Osudpotro**, **Lazz Pharma**, **ePharma**, **MedEasy**.
- Local licensed pharmacies.

## Verify the seller
- The pharmacy should display its **DGDA drug licence** number. If none is shown, treat it as unverified and say so.
- Never source medicine from Facebook pages, Bikroy, random Daraz sellers, or imports.

## Price checks
- Many medicines in Bangladesh carry an MRP set or approved by DGDA, printed on the pack. **A price above MRP is a red flag.** Note the MRP when you find it.
- Compare the exact brand, strength, form (tablet, capsule, syrup), and pack size the user named.

## Same-generic brands
If the user asks, you may list other brands with the **identical generic, strength, and form** and their prices, as a price comparison. Always add: "same generic, strength and form — ask your doctor or pharmacist before switching." Don't say which one to choose, and never list a different strength, form, or combination as equivalent.

## Check
- Expiry date on delivery.
- Prescription-only medicines: the pharmacy will require a prescription. Don't suggest ways around it.
- Cold-chain items (insulin, some vaccines): prefer a nearby pharmacy over long delivery.

## Out of scope
Diagnosing symptoms, interpreting prescriptions, and dosing for anyone, including children. Refer to a doctor or pharmacist.

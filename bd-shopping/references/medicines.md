# Medicines & health products

**Price and availability only.** Never recommend which medicine to take, a dose, or a substitute. That is a doctor's or pharmacist's call. If the user asks for one, say so and point them to their doctor or a pharmacist.

## Retailers
- Licensed online pharmacies: **Arogga**, **Osudpotro**, **Lazz Pharma**, **ePharma**, **MedEasy**.
- Local licensed pharmacies.

## Check
- Exact brand, strength, form, and pack size the user named.
- Expiry date on delivery.
- Seller is a licensed pharmacy.
- Prescription-only medicines: the pharmacy will require a prescription upload. Don't suggest ways around it.

## Never
- Source medicine from Facebook pages, Bikroy, or random Daraz sellers.
- Recommend importing medicine.

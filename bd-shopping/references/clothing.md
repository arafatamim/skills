# Clothing & footwear

## Retailers
- **Brand outlets and sites:** Aarong, Yellow, Ecstasy, Sailor, Le Reve, Richman (clothing); Bata, Apex (footwear).
- **Daraz** official brand stores.
- **Facebook pages** for boutique and local labels. Follow the Facebook rules in SKILL.md.

## Evidence
- Fabric composition, GSM (tees, knits), stitching photos.
- Owner complaints about shrinking, fading, pilling, and sizing. Star ratings are weak signal here.
- Fabric claims from FB pages and non-brand Daraz sellers ("100% cotton", "export quality") are the seller's claim, not fact. Say so.

## Fit
- Use **body and garment measurements in cm** from `shopping/prefs.md` (chest, waist, length, shoulder, inseam, foot length). Compare them against each listing's size chart.
- Never compare letter sizes (M, L, 40) across brands. They don't mean the same thing.
- If no measurements are stored, ask the user to measure a garment that fits well, and save the numbers.
- Note brand-specific fit the user reports ("Yellow L fits me, Richman runs small").

## Check
- **Return/exchange policy.** For online clothing this is often the deciding factor. No exchange = high risk.
- Whether the photos show the real product or catalogue/stock images.

## Timing
Pre-Eid is peak demand and peak price, except for brand-run campaigns. End-of-season sales are the best value. Imports carry sizing risk with no practical return path.

# Beauty, skincare & personal care

**Highest counterfeit risk of any category.** Only an authorized seller is real protection. Every other check can be faked.

## Retailers
- **Shajgoj** — specialist beauty retailer with online store and physical outlets; authorized partner for several international brands. Authorization is per brand: confirm it for the brand in question.
- Brand official stores on Daraz.
- Distributors the **brand itself** names for Bangladesh.
- Large supershops (Shwapno, Meena Bazar) for mass-market basics.

## Evidence
- Ingredient lists (INCIDecoder-style breakdowns).
- Dermatologist or independent reviews.
- Ignore influencer posts and sponsored "reviews".

## Authenticity checks — what they prove
| Check | A mismatch means | A match means |
|---|---|---|
| Batch code decoded by an online checker | Red flag | Nothing. Fakes copy real codes, and many brands aren't decodable |
| Importer sticker / hologram / verification code | Red flag | Weak evidence. Stickers are counterfeited |
| Sealed packaging | Red flag | Nothing |
| Price far below authorized sellers | Red flag | — |

Present these as red-flag screens, never as proof of authenticity.

## High-risk products
- **Perfume:** probably the most-faked beauty item. Authorized sellers only; treat any steep discount on a designer fragrance as fake until proven otherwise.
- **Sunscreen:** SPF/PA claims matter for health. Grey-imported Korean/Japanese sunscreens are common, often with no importer and uncertain storage. Prefer authorized sellers; flag heat exposure for grey stock.
- **Whitening/fairness creams:** skip unbranded or loose products. Mercury contamination has been documented in this segment in Bangladesh.

## Medical boundary
- **Strong actives:** prescription-strength retinoids (tretinoin), hydroquinone, steroid creams, and similar. Don't research or price them as cosmetics. Say they should be prescribed and supervised by a dermatologist.
- If the user mentions a reaction, a skin condition, pregnancy or breastfeeding in connection with a product, recommend a dermatologist or doctor rather than product-hunting.

## Fit
Skin type, sensitivities, and fragrance tolerance go in `shopping/prefs.md` once the user states them.

## Import notes
Cosmetics can be held at customs or require permits. Flag before recommending an import.

# Beauty, skincare & personal care

**Highest counterfeit risk of any category.** Default to authorized sellers only.

## Retailers
- **Shajgoj** — specialist beauty retailer with online store and physical outlets; authorized partner for several international brands.
- Brand official stores on Daraz.
- Authorized importers/distributors for specific brands.
- Large supershops (Shwapno, Meena Bazar) for mass-market basics.

## Evidence
- Ingredient lists (INCIDecoder-style breakdowns).
- Dermatologist or independent reviews.
- Ignore influencer posts and sponsored "reviews".

## Check
- **Importer sticker** and, where the brand uses them, hologram/verification codes.
- **Batch code** and manufacturing/expiry dates. Batch codes can be decoded with online checkers; flag mismatches between the code and the printed dates.
- Sealed packaging.
- Unbranded or loose "whitening" creams: skip. Contamination (e.g., mercury) has been a documented problem in this segment in Bangladesh.

## Fit
Skin type, sensitivities, and fragrance tolerance. Save to USER.md once known.

If the user mentions a reaction or a medical skin condition, recommend a dermatologist instead of product-hunting.

## Import notes
Cosmetics can be held at customs or require permits. Flag before recommending an import.

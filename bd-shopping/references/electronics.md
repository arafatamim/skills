# Electronics & computing

Phones, laptops, PC parts, peripherals, networking, wearables, cameras, audio.

## Retailers
- **Star Tech, Ryans, Techland** — PC parts, laptops, monitors, peripherals, networking. Static HTML; plain fetch usually works. Listings state warranty clearly.
- **Pickaboo, Apple Gadgets, Gadget & Gear** — phones, tablets, accessories. Check each listing for official vs. unofficial unit labelling.
- **Brand stores** (Xiaomi, Samsung, Realme, Walton, etc.) — reference price for official units.
- **Daraz** — official brand stores and DarazMall only.

## Evidence
RTINGS, Notebookcheck, GSMArena tests, Hardware Unboxed, Gamers Nexus. Owner reports for long-term reliability (hinges, batteries, boards, panels).

## Variants to match exactly
RAM/storage, CPU generation (i5-1335U ≠ i5-1235U), regional chip (Exynos vs. Snapdragon), global vs. China ROM, panel type.

## Timing
Check for an announced or credibly leaked successor in the next ~3 months. A big price drop on an older model often means stock clearance.

## Warranty
Official/brand vs. shop warranty vs. grey. Treat grey as no warranty.

## Phones and cellular devices
- Official vs. unofficial unit. Unofficial phones carry NEIR registration risk and may be blocked from local networks.
- Verify IMEI status through BTRC's NEIR service before buying a used or unofficial unit. Look up the current check method; don't rely on a remembered code.
- The official/unofficial price gap is usually the cost of that risk, not free savings.

## Import notes
Anything with a radio (Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, cellular) may need BTRC approval to clear customs.

# Baby: formula, gear, car seats, toys

Fakes plus physical safety risk. Bias every decision toward safety over price.

## Formula and baby food
- **Authorized sellers only:** brand-named distributors, licensed pharmacies, and large supershops. Never FB pages, Bikroy, or non-mall Daraz sellers.
- Check sealed tin, expiry, and batch on delivery. Heat-exposed grey stock is a risk.
- **Which formula a baby should have is a paediatrician's decision**, especially for allergies, reflux, prematurity, or special formulas. Research price and availability only.
- Compare price per 100 g only between identical products.

## Diapers and wipes
Mostly price: compare ৳ per piece for the same size. Check the size range by weight on the pack, not the size letter.

## Car seats
- Look for a certification label: UN ECE R44/04 or R129 (i-Size). No label → skip.
- Match to the child's weight/height range and to the car (ISOFIX or belt-fit).
- **Used car seats:** avoid unless the history is known. A seat that's been in a crash or is past its expiry date (many have one moulded in) can fail.

## Strollers, cribs, high chairs
Brakes, harness, stability, and folding locks. For cribs: slat spacing and mattress fit (no gaps). Check recalls for the model.

## Toys
- Follow the **age label**. For under-3s: no small parts.
- Treat **button/coin batteries** and **small high-powered magnets** as serious hazards. Battery compartments should need a screwdriver.
- Cheap unbranded painted toys: lead-paint risk. Prefer brands that state compliance with a recognised toy standard.

## Medical boundary
Feeding, sleep, and health questions go to a paediatrician. The agent prices and checks safety labels; it doesn't advise on infant care.

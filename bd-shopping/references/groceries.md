# Groceries & household consumables

## Retailers
- **Chaldal** — online grocer, fast delivery in Dhaka and several other cities.
- **Shwapno** — supershop chain with online store (also available through pandamart).
- **Meena Click** — Meena Bazar's online store.
- **Daraz Mart** — packaged and non-perishable items.
- **Khaas Food** — food-safety-focused grocer.

## Evidence
This category is mostly about price. **Compare unit price** (৳/kg, ৳/L, ৳/unit), never pack price.

## Check
- Pack size and unit price.
- Delivery fee and minimum order (free-delivery thresholds change often; check live).
- Expiry date for bulk buys vs. how fast the household will use it.
- BSTI mark on packaged food.

## Output
A short table sorted by unit price beats a BUY/WAIT verdict here. Note bulk-buy savings vs. shelf life.

Fresh produce: online quality varies by order. Mention that the user may prefer to inspect in person.
